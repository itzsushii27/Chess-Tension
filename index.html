Tension Lab

Attack measures how hard each side pushes into the other’s half of the board. Tension is high only when both sides are pushing, so a one-sided mate scores 0.

Every piece is scored on its own and the scores are added, so two pieces hitting one square count twice. Rooks multiply their points by 1.25 and queens by 1.5. Kings score nothing themselves, but the squares around a king are worth more.

A = White Attack + Black Attack. T = A − |White Attack − Black Attack|, which equals 2 × the smaller side’s attack. Like a rope pulled from both ends, tension is limited by the weaker pull.
